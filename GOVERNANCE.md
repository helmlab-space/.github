# Governance

This applies to every repository of the [helmlab-space](https://github.com/helmlab-space) organization.

## Maintainer

Helmlab is maintained by one person, Görkem Yıldız, who reviews and merges changes and publishes releases.

## Decisions

Lasting decisions are recorded as numbered architecture decision records in [helmlab/docs/adr](https://github.com/Grkmyldz148/helmlab/tree/main/docs/adr). A number is never reused; a reversed decision is marked superseded. A frozen definition, such as Helmlab 2026, never changes its outputs; work that would change them goes to a later definition.

## AI tools

Helmlab is maintained by one person who uses AI tools for drafting code, documentation and analyses. The maintainer reviews and approves every change before it is merged or published. AI tools are not credited as authors; the maintainer is responsible for all content.
